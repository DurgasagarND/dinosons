PRODUCTS & CARE

How long does the battery last?

Runtime depends on model, power mode and the attachment in use. The Rex V12 offers up to 90 minutes in eco mode under suitable conditions.

How do I care for the filters?

Follow the instructions supplied with your model. Let washable filters dry fully before reinstalling them.

Which model should I choose?

Compare suction, runtime and included features on our model comparison page, or contact support for help choosing.

ORDERS & WARRANTY

Do you deliver across India?

Delivery availability and timing depend on your location. Contact our team with your pin code to confirm options.

What warranty is included?

Dinoson products include a 5-year parts and labour warranty, subject to the terms provided with your purchase.

Can I return a product?

Eligible products can be returned within the 30-day home trial period. Please contact support for current return steps and conditions.
